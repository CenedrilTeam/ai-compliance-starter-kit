# Notice to employees on the use of AI systems

> **AI Compliance Starter Kit.** Template for informing workers' representatives and the affected workers under Art. 26(7) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 before a high-risk AI system is put into service at the workplace. Section 2 contains one subsection per system in use. Participation rights of workers' representatives under national law remain unaffected.

## 1. Why you are receiving this

[YOUR_ORGANISATION_NAME] uses AI systems that count as high-risk systems under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Before such a system is put into service at the workplace, [YOUR_ORGANISATION_NAME] informs the workers' representatives and the affected employees that they will be subject to its use (Article 26(7)).

This notice is that information. It sets out which systems are used, what for, who is affected and where to direct questions.

## 3. What this means for you

You work with these systems or are affected by their results. Those results are proposals that a person reviews before they become a decision. If a result strikes you as implausible, report it to the person named above; such feedback feeds into the monitoring of the system.

Your rights under employment law and data protection law are unaffected. In particular, you may ask for an explanation of a decision that is based on the output of such a system and significantly affects you.
