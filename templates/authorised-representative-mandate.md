# Mandate of the Authorised Representative in the Union

> **AI Compliance Starter Kit.** Contract template for providers established outside the Union that appoint an authorised representative under Art. 22 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. The text is a proposal for the written mandate; both parties negotiate and sign it.

## 2. Subject matter and legal basis

The provider is established outside the Union and makes high-risk AI systems available on the Union market. Under Article 22(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 it appoints, by written mandate and prior to making those systems available, an authorised representative established in the Union. By this mandate the representative accepts the appointment.

- [SYSTEM]

The representative performs the tasks entrusted to it in addition to the obligations of the provider. The responsibility of the provider under the Regulation remains unaffected (Article 22(4), second sentence).

## 3. Tasks of the representative

Under Article 22(3) the representative performs at least the following tasks:

- It verifies that the EU declaration of conformity and the technical documentation have been drawn up and that the provider has carried out the appropriate conformity assessment procedure.
- It keeps the contact details of the provider, a copy of the EU declaration of conformity, the technical documentation and, where applicable, the certificate of the notified body available for the competent authorities for ten years after the system has been placed on the market or put into service.
- It provides a competent authority, upon a reasoned request, with all the information and documentation necessary to demonstrate the conformity of a system, including access to the automatically generated logs to the extent they are under the control of the provider.
- It cooperates, upon a reasoned request, with the competent authorities on any action they take in relation to a high-risk AI system, in particular to reduce and mitigate the risks it poses.
- It complies with the registration obligations under Article 49 or, where the registration is carried out by the provider itself, verifies that the information referred to in Annex VIII, Section A, point 3 is correct.

The representative is the point of contact for the competent authorities on all questions related to compliance with the Regulation; it acts in addition to, or instead of, the provider (Article 22(4), first sentence).

## 4. Cooperation of the provider

The provider supplies the representative with the documents and information it needs for its tasks and keeps them current. These include the EU declaration of conformity, the technical documentation, the certificate of the notified body where applicable, the instructions for use and information on changes to the system.

The provider informs the representative without delay of serious incidents, of measures taken by a market surveillance authority, and of withdrawals or recalls affecting a system covered by this mandate.

## 5. Termination of the mandate

The representative terminates the mandate where it considers or has reason to consider that the provider acts contrary to its obligations under the Regulation (Article 22(4), third sentence). In that case it immediately informs the competent market surveillance authority and, where applicable, the notified body concerned about the termination and the reasons for it.

The retention duty under section 3 continues to apply to the systems covered up to the termination, to the extent the Regulation so requires.

## 6. Filing and evidence

The signed mandate is filed in the provider's document control; the filing location is noted with the details of the authorised representative.

## 7. Conclusion

The mandate is concluded in writing between [YOUR_ORGANISATION_NAME] and [REPRESENTATIVE_NAME] and signed by both parties.
