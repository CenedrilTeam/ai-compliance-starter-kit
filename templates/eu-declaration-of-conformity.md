# EU Declaration of Conformity

pursuant to Article 47 and Annex V of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

> **AI Compliance Starter Kit.** Template for the EU declaration of conformity of a high-risk AI system under Art. 47 and Annex V. The declaration is drawn up per system in writing, in machine-readable, physical or electronically signed form, translated into a language that can be easily understood by the national competent authorities of the Member States in which the system is made available, and kept for ten years. In section 7 the applicable variant remains. The texts follow the product's building blocks; name and type of the system (Annex V point 1) and name and function of the signatory (point 8) are added.
